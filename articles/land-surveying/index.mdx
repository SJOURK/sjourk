This handbook is a modern, interactive resource for learning specialised English in the field of land surveying, designed for Master's degree students at the B2/C1 level of proficiency. The content and interactive exercises have been developed by an English language instructor at the Foreign Languages Unit of the University of Agriculture in Kraków.
