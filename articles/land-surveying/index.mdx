This handbook is a modern, interactive resource for learning English for Specific Purposes in the field of land surveying. Intended for Master’s students at the B2/C1 level of English proficiency, it combines field-specific vocabulary with interactive exercises. The materials have been designed by an English language instructor at the Foreign Languages Unit of the University of Agriculture in Kraków.
