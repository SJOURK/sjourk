<DropZone answer="prime meridian"/>The imaginary line at 0° longitude, passing through Greenwich, England.

<DropZone answer="pole"/> A point where the Earth's axis of rotation meets its surface.

<DropZone answer="latitude"/>The angular distance of a place north or south of the equator.

<DropZone answer="geographic coordinate system"/>A framework of intersecting lines used to specify any location on the Earth's surface.

<DropZone answer="Tropic of Capricorn"/>The parallel of latitude at approximately 23.5° south, marking the southern edge of the tropics.

<DropZone answer="parallel"/>An imaginary line circling the Earth parallel to the equator, connecting points of equal latitude.

<DropZone answer="equator"/>The imaginary line circling the Earth exactly halfway between the two poles, at 0° latitude.

<DropZone answer="longitude"/>The angular distance of a place east or west of the Prime Meridian.
